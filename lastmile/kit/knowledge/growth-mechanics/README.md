# Growth Mechanics Knowledge Library

> Deeper references for you and your AI on the cheapest growth loops a launch-stage product gets — store reviews, referrals, and the onboarding path to activation. The center of gravity: the popular review-gating pattern is banned/risky, referrals come after retention, and every signup field before value is a toll booth.

The founder-facing companions are [`GROWTH_LOOPS_GUIDE.md`](../../guides/GROWTH_LOOPS_GUIDE.md) and [`ONBOARDING_GUIDE.md`](../../guides/ONBOARDING_GUIDE.md).

## How to use it

| When | How this library helps |
|---|---|
| Planning growth | Judge growth-loop plans (review prompting, referral economics, k-factor claims) against the rules. |
| Specifying flows | Write onboarding/activation and review-prompt flows that pass `GRW-*` by construction. |
| Final check | Flag review-gating violations and untested anonymous-linking paths in built features. |
